About the firm

Clients' needs first, every time.

Diamond Retirement Strategies is an independent agency led by Michele Diamond. For more than 20 years we have helped Valley families turn their savings into dependable retirement income.

We believe retirement income planning needs both a strategy and a process. We work only with companies that are financially sound and have a proven track record, and we look past income alone to Medicare, long-term care, life insurance and the legacy you leave.
