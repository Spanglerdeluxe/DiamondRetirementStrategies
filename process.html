Home/Our process

A clear path from first call to lifelong plan.

We believe retirement income planning needs both a strategy and a process. Here is exactly what working with us looks like.

Four steps

What happens, and when.

01

Discovery meeting

About 60 minutes · No cost

We get to know you: your goals, your concerns and the retirement you picture. There is nothing to sign and nothing to buy.

  • Your retirement timeline
  • What worries you most
  • What matters beyond money
02

Retirement review

1 to 2 weeks

We gather your statements and analyze every income source, account, policy and expense to build one complete picture.

  • Social Security and pension estimates
  • Account fees and risk
  • Existing insurance coverage
03

Your income plan

Second meeting

We walk you through a written plan that shows where your income will come from each year and how it is protected.

  • Year-by-year income map
  • Options compared side by side
  • Time to ask every question
04

Put it in place and review

Ongoing

Once you decide, Carrie handles applications and transfers. Then we meet at least once a year to keep your plan current.

  • Paperwork handled for you
  • Annual review meeting
  • Updates when life changes

Getting ready

What to bring to your first meeting.

Don't worry if you can't find everything. Bring what you have and we will help with the rest.

  • Recent statements for 401(k), 403(b), TSP, IRA and brokerage accounts
  • Social Security statement (download it from ssa.gov/myaccount)
  • Pension estimates and payout options from your employer
  • Insurance policies: life, long-term care and any annuities
  • Your Medicare card or current health insurance details
  • Last year's tax return
  • A rough monthly budget, or your best estimate of spending
Meeting options: we can meet at our office, by video call or by phone, whichever is most comfortable for you.
