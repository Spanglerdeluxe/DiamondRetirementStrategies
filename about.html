Our story

Over two decades of turning savings into safety.

Diamond Retirement Strategies was founded on a simple idea: people who spent a lifetime saving deserve a clear plan for their retirement.

Michele Diamond started the company to give pre-retirees and retirees a place to get straight answers about their retirement. For more than 25 years, the practice has grown by referral, one family at a time, by putting each client's needs ahead of any product.

Today we help individuals, married couples and families with retirement planning, rollovers, safe investments, life insurance and legacy planning. We also offer DRS Estate Planning, so clients can have a full and complete plan.

Our belief: retirement planning needs both a strategy and a process. A strategy tells you where you are going. A process makes sure you get there and stay on course.

Why independent matters

We work for you, not for one company.

As an independent agency, we are not tied to any single company. That lets us compare options from many sources and choose what fits your personal situation.

  • Carefully chosen partners. We work only with companies that are financially sound and have a proven track record in their industry.
  • Side-by-side comparisons. When there are several good choices, we show you the trade-offs so you can choose with confidence.
  • One relationship. Retirement planning, life insurance and estate planning are handled by the same team who knows your whole picture.
