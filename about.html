Our story

Two decades of turning savings into income.

Diamond Retirement Strategies was founded on a simple idea: people who spent a lifetime saving deserve a clear plan for spending it.

Michele Diamond started the firm to give pre-retirees and retirees in the Phoenix area a place to get straight answers about retirement income. Over more than 20 years, the practice has grown by referral, one family at a time, by putting each client's needs ahead of any product.

Today we help individuals, married couples and families with retirement income planning, rollovers, Medicare and health coverage, long-term care, life insurance and legacy planning. Through our sister practice, DRS Estate Planning, clients can also put their wills and trusts in order.

Our belief: retirement income planning needs both a strategy and a process. A strategy tells you where you are going. A process makes sure you get there and stay on course.

What we stand for

Passion, trustworthiness and accountability.

These three values guide every meeting, every recommendation and every follow-up call.

Passion

We love this work

Helping someone retire with confidence is the best part of our day. We bring that energy to every plan, whether your savings are modest or substantial.

Trustworthiness

Plain answers, no surprises

We explain how each option works, what it costs and what it does not do. You will always know why we recommend something before you decide.

Accountability

We stay with you

A plan is only as good as its follow-through. We meet regularly, answer the phone and take responsibility for the advice we give.

Why independent matters

We work for you, not for one company.

As an independent agency, we are not tied to a single insurance company. That lets us compare options from many carriers and choose what fits your situation.

  • Carefully chosen partners. We work only with companies that are financially sound and have a proven track record in their industry.
  • Side-by-side comparisons. When there are several good choices, we show you the trade-offs so you can choose with confidence.
  • One relationship. Income, Medicare, long-term care and life insurance are handled by the same local team who knows your whole picture.
