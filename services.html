Retirement Income Planning

Income planningFixed indexed annuitiesSocial Security maximization

Your paycheck stops when you retire. Your bills do not. We build a plan that turns your savings, Social Security and any pension into reliable monthly income.

We start with your real expenses, then map each income source against them year by year. Where there is a gap, we look at ways to fill it, including fixed indexed annuities that can provide income you cannot outlive while protecting your principal from market downturns.

When should I claim Social Security?Claiming at 62, at full retirement age or at 70 can change your lifetime benefit by tens of thousands of dollars.
How much can I safely spend?We test your plan against market drops, inflation and a long life.
Is an annuity right for me?We explain how each type works, its costs and limits, and whether it fits at all.
What if one of us passes first?We plan for survivor income so a spouse is not left short.

Rollovers & Retirement Accounts

401(k) rolloversIRAsIRA rollovers403(b) planningThrift Savings Plans

Leaving an employer brings a big decision: what to do with the money in your workplace plan.

We compare your options side by side, including leaving funds where they are, rolling into an IRA or using part of the balance for guaranteed income. When a rollover makes sense, Carrie handles the paperwork so money moves directly between institutions and avoids unnecessary taxes or penalties.

Should I roll over my 401(k)?Not always. We weigh fees, investment choices and protections before recommending a move.
What about my TSP or 403(b)?Federal and education plans have their own rules. We know them well.
How do I avoid the 20% withholding?A direct trustee-to-trustee transfer avoids it. We set that up for you.
Can I combine old accounts?Consolidating can simplify RMDs and make your plan easier to manage.

Medicare & Health Coverage

Medicare supplementsHealth insuranceMedicaid planning

Healthcare is one of the largest costs in retirement. The right coverage protects both your health and your income plan.

We help you enroll on time, compare Medicare supplement plans, and review your coverage each year. If you retire before 65, we help bridge the gap with individual health insurance. For families facing nursing-home costs, we explain Medicaid planning options and the rules that apply in Arizona.

When do I sign up?Your initial window is seven months around your 65th birthday. Missing it can mean lifelong penalties.
Supplement or Advantage?We explain the trade-offs between flexibility, cost and networks.
What if I keep working past 65?Employer coverage changes the rules. We help you time enrollment correctly.
How does Medicaid planning work?We explain eligibility and look-back rules so you can plan ahead.

Long-Term Care

Long-term care insuranceHybrid life & LTCAnnuity care riders

Most people turning 65 will need some form of long-term care. Planning ahead keeps that care from draining your savings or burdening your family.

We compare traditional long-term care insurance with hybrid policies that combine life insurance or annuities with care benefits. Together we decide how much protection you need, how to pay for it and what happens if you never use it.

Doesn't Medicare cover this?Medicare covers only short skilled-care stays, not ongoing custodial care.
What if I never need care?Hybrid policies can pass a benefit to heirs if care is never used.
When should I buy?Coverage is easier to qualify for and usually costs less in your 50s and early 60s.
Can I care for a parent?We help adult children plan for a parent's care costs too.

Life Insurance & Legacy

Life insuranceLegacy planning529 college savings

What you leave behind matters as much as what you live on. We help you protect the people you love and pass on your savings the way you intend.

We review the life insurance you already own, check that every beneficiary designation is current, and look for ways to leave more to heirs and causes you care about. For grandparents, 529 college savings plans are a simple way to help the next generation.

Do I still need life insurance?It can replace a pension for a surviving spouse or create a tax-efficient inheritance.
Are my beneficiaries up to date?Beneficiary forms override a will. We check every account.
Can I help pay for college?A 529 plan grows tax-free for qualified education costs.
How do I leave money to charity?We explain options that can lower taxes while supporting causes you love.

Tax & Business Strategies

Tax-efficient strategiesBusiness succession planning

It is not what you earn in retirement, it is what you keep. We look for ways to reduce taxes on your income over your whole retirement.

We plan the order in which you draw from taxable, tax-deferred and tax-free accounts, prepare for required minimum distributions and consider Roth conversions in lower-income years. For business owners, we help plan a succession or exit that funds your retirement. We work alongside your CPA and attorney.

Which account should I spend first?Withdrawal order can change your lifetime tax bill.
Should I convert to a Roth?Conversions can make sense before RMDs and Social Security begin.
How do RMDs affect me?We plan ahead so required withdrawals don't push you into a higher bracket.
How do I exit my business?We help plan buy-sell funding and a transition timeline.
